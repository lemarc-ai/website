1. Who we are

Lemarc ("we", "us", "our") is a marketing workspace for businesses. Lemarc is operated by Matan Buganim, based in Israel, who is the controller of the personal data described here except where section 4 says otherwise.

For anything in this policy, write to support@lemarc.ai.

2. What we collect

2.1 Your account

Your login email address, the access credentials for the accounts you connect, the material you add to your workspace, and records of how the service ran for you.

2.2 Accounts you connect

You choose which accounts to connect, and you can skip or disconnect any of them. Reading and acting are separate permissions: Lemarc publishes content or creates ads only in accounts you connect for that, and only for what you approve. Ads it creates stay switched off until you launch them.

2.3 Visitors and applicants

If you apply for early access on lemarc.ai, we collect your name, work email, LinkedIn URL and, if you tick the box, your consent to hear from us. If you ask for access inside the app, we collect your email address and, optionally, your name, company and website. Cookies on lemarc.ai are covered in section 9.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for (performance of our contract with you): analyzing your marketing, recommending next steps, producing material, and publishing or sending it where you tell us to.
  • To keep the service secure and working (our legitimate interests).
  • To review early access applications and reply (steps you asked us to take before a contract), and to send occasional updates if you opted in (your consent, which you can withdraw through the unsubscribe link in every message).
  • Site analytics and advertising (your consent, see section 9).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. Your data is not used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's. A person at Lemarc does not read your connected data except when you ask us to for support, when the law requires it, or when necessary for security. Lemarc makes no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Who receives it

Our processors. Service providers process data for us under contracts that limit them to providing their service: cloud hosting and database, AI model providers, speech-to-text, web page reading and search data services, and email delivery. For lemarc.ai: site hosting, our CRM (which receives applications), and the analytics and advertising services in section 9 if you allow them. A current list of these providers is available on request at support@lemarc.ai.

Accounts you connect. The platforms you connect receive what you ask Lemarc to send them, under their own terms with you.

Your customers' data. When you connect a CRM, you are the controller of your contacts' data and Lemarc processes it on your instructions. We will sign a data processing agreement on request and help you answer your contacts' requests.

Google user data. Lemarc's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the features described here, do not use it for advertising, do not transfer it except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, and do not let people read it except with your permission, where required by law, or for security.

Meta and LinkedIn data. We use data from Meta's and LinkedIn's APIs only to provide the features described here, in line with their platform terms. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, sales or recruiting, build profiles or audience lists from it, or transfer it to anyone other than our processors acting on our instructions.

5. Clip to Lemarc (browser extension)

Clip to Lemarc is an optional Chrome extension that saves what you read online into your Lemarc workspace. It runs only when you click its button, press its shortcut or choose it from the right-click menu. It then reads the text you selected, or the page's readable text, plus the page's address and title, and sends them to your Lemarc workspace. It keeps the capture in your browser only until Lemarc receives it, then deletes it. It does not read your browsing history, cookies or other tabs, stores no login, and uses what it captures only to save your clips. Nothing it captures is sold.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account is open, because the service relies on your history. Credentials are kept until you disconnect the account they belong to. Early access applications are kept for up to 24 months. Everything else is deleted within 30 days of closing your account, except records we must keep by law.

To remove data:

  1. Disconnect an account in Lemarc's settings. We delete its credentials and stop reading from it immediately, and ask the platform to revoke access where it supports that. Data already imported stays until you ask us to delete it. You can also remove Lemarc's access from the platform's own settings.
  2. Delete specific data, for example everything from one platform: email support@lemarc.ai. We will confirm when it is done.
  3. Delete everything: email support@lemarc.ai with the subject "delete my account". We will delete your workspace, credentials, imported data and account within 30 days and confirm in writing.

If you connected Lemarc through Meta, option 2 or 3 removes your Meta data.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each customer's data is kept apart from every other customer's, and access to production is limited to people who need it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy in a portable format. These come from the GDPR in the EU and UK, the Protection of Privacy Law in Israel, and the CCPA in California, which also protects you from discrimination for using them. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

Email support@lemarc.ai and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Requests about your customers' data are passed to you as its controller.

9. Cookies

The Lemarc app uses only cookies it needs to work, such as keeping you signed in. It has no analytics or advertising cookies.

lemarc.ai uses two optional kinds, each off until you allow it:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_<id>), to see how people find and use the site.
  • Marketing: Google Ads (_gcl_au), Meta Pixel (_fbp, _fbc) and LinkedIn Insight Tag (li_fat_id), to measure and target our ads. Those companies may also set cookies on their own sites.

Rejecting is as easy as accepting. We store your choice in a cookie named lemarc_consent for one year. Change it any time through Cookie settings at the bottom of every page.

10. Where data is stored

Your workspace data is stored in the EU. Lemarc is operated from Israel, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection, and some of our processors operate in the United States. For transfers not covered by an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. Children

Lemarc is a business tool and is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what we do with data, we will update this page and its date. If the change is significant, we will email account holders before it takes effect.
